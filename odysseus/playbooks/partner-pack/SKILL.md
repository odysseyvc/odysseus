name: partner-pack
description: "Putting together the pack for the firm's partner or pipeline meeting: when the owner asks for the Monday pack, the partner meeting pack, the agenda for the investment meeting, or what to bring to the partners."
---

One document the owner can read in five minutes and put in front of the partners. Facts come from the firm's own records; the only writing of your own is a short take on each new deal, marked as yours.

Gather:
1. pipeline_review, once. Its lists and figures are worked out by code: use them as they are. Do not recount the pipeline yourself.
2. For each entry it lists as new this week, and each one a decision is needed on (see below): the record in the database, the latest notes and the deck if one is kept. Read enough for a paragraph, no more.
3. portfolio_show, if any company's figures have been recorded, for what it points out.
4. The owner's calendar for the meeting itself, if they did not say when it is.

A decision is needed on an entry when its stage or its next step says so (a partner vote, an investment committee date, a term sheet out or awaited), or when the owner names it. If you cannot tell, list it under "Is a decision needed?" and do not guess.

The pack, in this order:
1. This week in one line: open, new, gone quiet, overdue, owed a reply, from the review.
2. Decisions needed: each entry with what is to be decided, the round and amount on record, and what is still missing to decide it.
3. New this week: each with one paragraph. First what the company does and where it came from, from the record. Then one or two sentences headed "My take:", which are yours, say what you would want to know first, and say when you had only a record and no deck to go on.
4. Needs chasing: overdue next steps, entries gone quiet, people owed a reply, as the review gives them, with whose each is where the record says.
5. Portfolio: only what portfolio_show points out. Leave the section out if nothing is recorded.

Rules:
- Every fact with its source in brackets, briefly: (database), (deck), (call of 14 Oct), (review).
- A take is never written as the firm's view or the owner's. Do not recommend investing or passing.
- If the review says it took some stages as closed, repeat that line at the foot, so a wrong guess is seen.
- Keep it to two pages unless the owner asks for more.

The file: a PDF, made by writing one web page and printing it, as the instructions for documents say; a Word file instead if the owner has a document template and asks for Word. Look at a trial of the pages before sending. In the chat, three lines: what needs deciding, what is new, what is overdue. Send it to nobody but the owner unless they ask, and then through the usual approval.
